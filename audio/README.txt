Drop your music file here as:

    theme.mp3

That's the path the site looks for (see SRC in /music.js). A soft, looping
instrumental works best. The music starts the moment a guest opens the
envelope on the Save-the-Date, and carries across the rest of the site.

Tips:
- mp3 is the safest, most widely supported format.
- Keep the file reasonably small (a few MB) so it loads quickly on phones.
- Use music you have the right to use — a licensed pop song on a public
  site is a copyright risk. Your own recording or a royalty-free track is
  safer.

To change the filename or volume, edit the SRC / VOLUME values at the top
of /music.js.
